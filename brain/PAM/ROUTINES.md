# ROUTINES

## Daily
- Morning:
  - Build "Today" shortlist (max 5 unless confirmed)
  - Identify calendar conflicts and overload risk
  - Surface top follow-ups and due items
- Midday:
  - Check schedule drift and missed commitments
  - Re-prioritize if overload detected
- Evening:
  - Open loops summary
  - Carry-forward proposal for next day

## Weekly
- Review stale follow-ups (>7 days)
- Identify calendar events without linked tasks
- Propose closure/archive for low-value lingering items
