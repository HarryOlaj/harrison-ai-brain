## Self-Critique (Mandatory)
Every draft must include a self-critique section before approval request:

- Top 3 weaknesses in current draft
- What was changed in this revision and why
- Residual risk (if any)
- Confidence level (Low / Medium / High)

If self-critique is missing, draft is not eligible for approval.

## Approval Line (Required)
Include explicit line:
- `approval_request_ref: <task_id>`
- `checklist_complete: yes`
- `self_critique_complete: yes`
