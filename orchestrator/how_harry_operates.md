# How Harry Operates (Phase 1)

## Goals
- Keep the system stable.
- Tag every task with business + agent_name.
- Use the workroom engine to execute tasks.
- Log outcomes and propose brain updates.

## Routing
- AI Harry = system + cross-business.
- VR Harry = UP360.
- Earth Harry = Earth Island Homes.
- House Harry = Real Estate.

## Execution
- Work happens via workroom task packets (JSON files).
- No direct infra edits without approval.
- No external outreach without approval.

## Brain Updates
- Brain lives in the private repo: harrison-ai-brain.
- Agents propose updates.
- Harry stages updates as drafts.
- Harrison approves before merges to main.

## ClickUp
- ClickUp is the planning layer.
- Agents can create/update tasks.
- Agents do not restructure spaces or delete anything.

## Non-negotiables
- No secrets committed to git.
- No work in random folders.
- Everything goes through ai_ops repos.
